# The tour of the app.
#
# The points are for a screen of 1080 wide and 1920 high. They are scaled to
# the screen of the device that is used, so the same tour works on any phone.
#
# A step is one of:
#
#   tap    X Y   [name]        a tap on a point of the screen
#   taptext  words [name]      a tap on the text that is shown
#   swipe  X1 Y1 X2 Y2 [ms] [name]   a swipe from one point to another
#   text   words              typing into the field that is open
#   key    KEYCODE            a key, e.g. BACK, ENTER
#   shot   name               a picture without touching the screen
#   wait   seconds            a pause
#
# The tour goes through the whole app: the list, a new file, the editor, the
# settings and the themes.
#
# The taps are done by name wherever the app gives a name, because a name
# keeps working when the layout moves. The points are only used where the
# app shows nothing that can be named.

# --- the list, with nothing in it yet ---
shot 01-list-empty
wait 2

# --- a new file ---
# The + button in the corner, bottom right.
taptext "New file" 02-tap-new-file
wait 3
shot 03-editor-empty
wait 1

# The title of the file. The field says "Title" when it is empty.
taptext "Title" 04-title-field
wait 2
text Shopping list
wait 2
shot 05-title-typed
wait 1

# The body of the file. The field says "Write your file…" when it is empty.
taptext "Write your file…" 06-body-field
wait 2
text Milk
text Bread
text Coffee
wait 2
shot 07-body-typed
wait 1

# Save it with the check mark, top right.
taptext "Save" 08-save
wait 3
shot 09-list-with-one-file
wait 1

# --- the settings and the themes ---
# The gear in the top bar.
taptext "Settings" 10-open-settings
wait 3
shot 11-settings
wait 1

# The themes are further down the settings, so scroll to them.
swipe 540 1500 540 700 400 12-scroll-to-themes
wait 2
shot 13-themes
wait 1

# --- back to the list ---
taptext "Back" 14-back-from-the-settings
wait 3
shot 15-back-at-the-list
wait 1

# --- the search ---
taptext "Search all files" 16-tap-search
wait 2
text shopping
wait 3
shot 17-search
wait 1
taptext "Clear search" 18-clear-search
wait 2
shot 19-search-cleared
wait 1

# --- the file again, through its row ---
taptext "Shopping list" 20-open-the-file
wait 3
shot 21-file-open
wait 1
taptext "Back" 22-back-from-the-file
wait 2
shot 23-at-the-end
