## What the log does not contain, and why that matters

No `FAILED`, no `ERROR`, no refused write, and no row the app could not name. **A trace with eleven
opinions and no faults is what a session looks like when the app is working and the design is not
finished** — which is the difference between these reports and the ones in the v129 log, where the
trace itself was the evidence.
